SACRED MANTRAS & CHANTS

Maha Mantra

हरे कृष्ण हरे कृष्ण कृष्ण कृष्ण हरे हरे | हरे राम हरे राम राम राम हरे हरे ||

"O Lord Krishna, O Energy of the Lord (Hara/Radha), please engage me in Your transcendental loving service."

Radha Gayatri Mantra

ॐ वृषभानुजायै विद्महे कृष्णप्रियायै धीमहि तन्नो राधा प्रचोदयात् ||

"May the daughter of King Vrishabhanu and the beloved of Krishna illuminate our intellect and guide our spiritual path."

Krishna Shloka (Bhagavad Gita 4.7)

यदा यदा हि धर्मस्य ग्लानिर्भवति भारत | अभ्युत्थानमधर्मस्य तदात्मानं सृजाम्यहम् ||

"Whenever virtue subsides and wickedness prevails, I manifest Myself on earth."
